## Expected behaviour

Treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination and retaliation are prohibited. These expectations apply in offices, online, during business travel and at work-related social events.

## Gifts and hospitality

A non-cash gift or hospitality worth up to and including £50 may be accepted only if it cannot reasonably influence a business decision. Record it in the Gifts Register within 5 working days. Anything above £50 requires written approval from Compliance before acceptance. Cash and gift cards must always be declined, regardless of value.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare an actual or potential conflict to compliance@northstar-demo.example before participating in the affected decision. Examples include selecting a supplier owned by a relative or paid outside work for a competitor. Compliance decides the controls required; declaring a conflict does not automatically make the activity permissible.

## Raising concerns

Report conduct concerns to Priya Shah at people@northstar-demo.example. If the concern involves Priya, contact Alex Morgan at security@northstar-demo.example. Retaliation for raising a concern in good faith is prohibited. The policy does not promise that every report can remain anonymous.

## Relationship to other policies

Use the expenses policy to claim permitted business spending and the information security policy to report data incidents. An expense approval does not override the gifts or conflicts rules.
