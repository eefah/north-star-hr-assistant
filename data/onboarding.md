## Before day one

The People team sends the contract and right-to-work checklist. Return the requested documents at least 5 working days before your start date. Do not email identity documents to a colleague; use the secure link supplied by the People team.

## Day one

Your line manager meets you at reception at 09:30 and introduces your buddy. IT issues your managed laptop and headset, sets up multi-factor authentication and tests your access. If an account is unavailable, raise a ServiceDesk ticket with category NEW-STARTER; the target first response is 4 working hours, not a guaranteed resolution time.

## First week

Complete information security and code of conduct training within 5 working days of starting. Your line manager agrees your first-month goals by the end of working day 5. Read the annual leave, sickness absence and expenses policies before making related requests or claims.

## First month

Your line manager holds a check-in at the end of weeks 1, 2 and 4. Permanent UK employees may request hybrid working after their first 4 weeks, subject to role suitability and written line-manager approval under the remote working policy. The company home-office allowance is not an automatic joining bonus.

## Contacts and scope

For onboarding questions contact people@northstar-demo.example. Use security@northstar-demo.example for suspected security incidents and the 1-hour reporting deadline in the information security policy. This document does not contain live account status, individual salaries or probation outcomes.
