## Salary bands

Northstar Studio uses five salary bands for permanent UK employees. Band 1 is £26,000 to £32,000. Band 2 is £32,000 to £42,000. Band 3 is £42,000 to £55,000. Band 4 is £55,000 to £72,000. Band 5 is £72,000 to £95,000. Contractors are outside the band structure.

## Starting salaries

Offer new starters a salary within the band for their role. An offer above the band midpoint requires written approval from Priya Shah, Head of People, before the offer is sent. An offer outside the band range requires approval from both Priya Shah and Daniel Reed, Finance Operations Lead.

## Annual pay review

The annual pay review takes effect on 1 April. Finance confirms the review budget by 31 January. Line managers submit pay proposals in PeopleHub by 28 February; People administrators check them against the bands before approval. Employees who joined after 1 January are not included in that year's review.

## Confidentiality

Band ranges and individual salaries are confidential People team records. Do not share band ranges with employees or line managers outside a formal offer or pay review. An employee may ask the People team for their own salary and band; refer all other salary questions to people@northstar-demo.example.
