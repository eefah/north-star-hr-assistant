## Scope and eligibility

This policy describes fictional enhanced company benefits for permanent UK employees with at least 26 weeks of continuous service by the expected week of birth or placement. Contractors are outside this policy. It does not provide a complete statement of statutory entitlements; ask the People team about statutory rights or eligibility outside this enhancement.

## Primary carer benefit

An eligible primary carer may take up to 26 weeks under this enhanced scheme: 12 weeks at full base pay followed by 14 weeks without company pay. Any statutory payment and interaction with this enhancement must be confirmed by the People team. This document does not specify statutory payment rates.

## Partner benefit

An eligible partner may take 4 weeks at full base pay. Take the leave in one block within 6 months of birth or placement. Only one employee may use the primary carer benefit for the same child; the other may use the partner benefit if eligible.

## How to request

Notify the People team at least 15 weeks before the expected birth date, or within 7 calendar days of receiving adoption placement notification. Provide the expected date and proposed leave dates. The People team confirms eligibility and the pay schedule in writing.

## Support and return

Contact Priya Shah at people@northstar-demo.example for confidential planning. Discuss your return arrangements at least 4 weeks before returning. Requests for hybrid working are assessed under the remote working policy; parental leave approval does not automatically approve a remote working pattern.
