## Scope and informal stage

This procedure guides line managers handling suspected misconduct by permanent UK employees. Contractors are managed under their engagement agreement. For minor first concerns, hold an informal conversation and record a summary in PeopleHub within 2 working days. An informal conversation is not a warning.

## Investigation

Contact the People team before starting any formal action. The People team appoints an investigator within 5 working days. The investigator must not be the employee's line manager or a witness. The employee may be suspended on full base pay only with approval from Priya Shah, Head of People; suspension is not a disciplinary sanction.

## Disciplinary hearing

Give the employee written notice of the hearing at least 3 working days in advance, including the allegations and the evidence. The employee may be accompanied by a work colleague or a trade union representative. A hearing may be rescheduled once, by up to 5 working days, if the companion is unavailable.

## Outcomes

Possible outcomes are no action, a first written warning, a final written warning or dismissal. A first written warning stays active for 6 months and a final written warning for 12 months. Gross misconduct may lead to dismissal without prior warnings; examples include harassment or retaliation under the code of conduct and deliberate disclosure of customer data under the information security policy.

## Appeals

The employee may appeal in writing to people@northstar-demo.example within 5 working days of receiving the outcome letter. A manager who was not involved in the investigation or hearing hears the appeal. The appeal decision is final under this procedure.

## Contact

Procedure owner: Priya Shah, Head of People. Line managers should contact people@northstar-demo.example before any formal step. This document does not contain individual case records.
