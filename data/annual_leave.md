## Eligibility

Permanent UK employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus England and Wales bank holidays. Part-time entitlement is pro-rated by contracted weekly hours. Contractors are outside this policy. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## How to request

Submit requests in PeopleHub at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Your line manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Do not treat a submitted request as approved. Requests for more than 10 consecutive working days also need approval from Priya Shah, Head of People.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year with line-manager approval recorded by 15 December. Carried days must be used by 31 March. This demo policy does not describe statutory exceptions or individual contractual arrangements; refer those questions to the People team.

## Illness during leave

If you become ill during approved annual leave, notify your line manager by 10:00 on the first affected working day and follow the sickness reporting policy. The People team reviews requests to reclassify affected days. Reclassification is not automatic.
