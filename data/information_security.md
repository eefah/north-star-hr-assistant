## Devices and access

Use a company-managed laptop with device encryption and multi-factor authentication for company systems. Use the company VPN when working remotely. Never share passwords or one-time authentication codes. Lock your screen whenever leaving your device unattended.

## Lost devices and incidents

Report a lost laptop, suspected phishing, accidental disclosure or unauthorised access to security@northstar-demo.example within 1 hour of discovery. If you cannot use company email, call the fictional internal service desk on extension 4400. Do not wait until the next working day. The security team coordinates containment; do not attempt your own remote wipe.

## Customer data

Store customer information only in approved company systems. Do not copy it to personal email, personal cloud storage or removable USB drives. Access is based on the minimum permissions required for your role. Questions about specific retention periods go to the security team; no retention schedule is included here.

## Use of AI tools

Only the approved Northstar Assist workspace may process internal company information. Do not enter customer personal data, credentials or payment details into any generative AI tool, including Northstar Assist. Public AI tools may be used only with public or synthetic information. This demo pack is synthetic.
