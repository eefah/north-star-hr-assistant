## Reporting

If you cannot work because of illness, telephone your line manager by 10:00 on the first affected working day. If they are unavailable, email people@northstar-demo.example by the same deadline. Tell them your likely absence duration if known; you do not need to disclose your diagnosis to colleagues.

## Evidence

Self-certify absences of up to 7 consecutive calendar days in PeopleHub. For more than 7 consecutive calendar days, provide a fit note covering the absence. Weekends count when determining whether a fit note is required.

## Company sick pay

Permanent UK employees who have passed probation receive up to 10 working days of company sick pay at full base pay per calendar year. Employees still on probation should ask the People team about their pay arrangements. Statutory sick pay eligibility, rates and interaction with company pay are not specified here.

## Return to work

Your line manager arranges a return-to-work conversation within 2 working days of your return. Discuss any support or temporary adjustments with the People team. Do not enter private medical details into a public team channel.

## Illness on holiday

For illness during approved annual leave, use the same first-day reporting deadline and evidence rules. The People team reviews reclassification of annual leave; see the annual leave policy. This document does not guarantee that every affected day will be restored.
