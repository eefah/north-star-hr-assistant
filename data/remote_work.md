## Eligibility

Permanent UK employees whose roles can be performed remotely may request hybrid working after completing their first 4 weeks. Hybrid arrangements require written line-manager approval. Contractors must use their engagement agreement; this policy does not grant them hybrid eligibility.

## Office attendance

The standard arrangement is at least 2 office days per working week, agreed with your line manager. Employees working fewer than 5 days per week agree an attendance pattern individually; this policy gives no automatic pro-rata formula. Team meetings may require attendance on a specified day.

## Location and availability

Work remotely only from your registered UK home address or another UK location approved in writing by your line manager. Working overseas is not permitted under this policy, including during a holiday. Be available between 10:00 and 15:00 on working days, excluding your agreed lunch break.

## Equipment and expenses

IT supplies a managed laptop and one headset. The annual home-office allowance is up to £150 per calendar year for an approved chair, keyboard or monitor. Obtain written line-manager approval before purchase and submit the claim under the expenses policy. Internet subscriptions, electricity and personal phone contracts are not reimbursed.

## Security

Use the company VPN and managed laptop for company systems. Do not use a shared family computer. Follow the information security policy if equipment is lost or information is disclosed.
