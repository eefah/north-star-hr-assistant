## Eligibility and submission

Employees may claim reasonable, necessary business expenses through SpendTrack. Submit claims within 30 calendar days of the purchase date. Attach an itemised receipt and state the business purpose. A missing receipt requires written approval from Finance before submission; a bank statement alone is not an itemised receipt.

## Approval and payment

Your line manager approves claims up to and including £500. Claims above £500 also need Finance approval from Daniel Reed. Approved claims are paid in the next Friday payment run if Finance receives them by Tuesday at 12:00; later claims move to the following Friday. This policy cannot show whether an individual payment has been made.

## Travel and meals

Book standard-class rail travel. A business-trip evening meal may be claimed up to £30 per person, including service charges. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Normal home-to-office commuting is not reimbursed, including the office days required by the remote working policy. Taxis require advance line-manager approval unless there is an immediate safety concern; explain any exception in the claim.

## Home-office purchases

Apply the eligibility, approval and £150 annual limit in the remote working policy before submitting a home-office claim. The £30 meal limit does not apply to home-office equipment. Purchases without the required advance approval are referred to Finance for review; payment is not guaranteed.
